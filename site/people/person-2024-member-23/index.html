Bina Singh

Biography from 2024

Prof. Bina Singh born and brought up in Varanasi, obtained her M. A. PhD in English from BHU Varanasi. Prof. Singh, former head of the department of English Vasant Kanya Mahavidyalaya BHU Varanasi. Presently Ms Singh is President of Kashi Tatav Sabha Theosophical Society Indian section Varanasi. Prof. Singh taught English literature and language for 40 years. Her area of interest is American literature, feminist literature Indian literature and other world literature. Dr. Singh has chaired participated and organized various national and international seminars conferences, literary festival and workshops. She has published 8 books, 3 on literature and 5 anthology of poems. Prof. Singh has received many national and international awards and recognition for her service to literature and humanity. Dr. Bina Singh is member of various academic and socio cultural organizations. Dr. Singh is patron of Vaidik foundation Kashi and Ashav, war against cancer. Literature is her passion and poetry first love. She believes that serving the suffering is serving the Supreme.
