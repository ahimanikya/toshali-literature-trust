Nidhi Garg

Biography from 2024

Nidhi Garga Dr. Nidhi Garg is a trained counselling psychologist with a master' s degree in counselling psychology and has Ph.D. in Mental Well-being of corporate employees. She runs her clinic in Bhubaneswar. Dr. Garg has more than a decade long experience in academia industry, social and personality development. She continues to contribute through media & literary interactions to empower adolescent and young women and men. She is also a guest faculty with number of institutes like NIFT and KIIT university. Apart from her interest in academics she is a published poet. She is the Ehsaas women of Bhubaneswar of Prabha Khaitan Foundation which runs literary programs in India and abroad.
