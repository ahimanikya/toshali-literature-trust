Toshali Literature Trust

Our Story

An open courtyard for literature—rooted in heritage, welcoming new voices.

Our purpose

Literature, learning
and cultural life.

Our purpose is to advance charitable, non-profit literary, educational and cultural work: encouraging the creation and sharing of literature, supporting learning, and bringing people together through language and the arts.

What we aim to do

Our objectives

The Trust’s objectives are to:

  • Promote literature, poetry, writing, publishing and literary research.
  • Organise literary events, seminars, workshops, readings and cultural programmes.
  • Encourage education, language studies and the creative arts.
  • Publish literature-related books, journals, magazines and digital content.
  • Support writers, scholars and artists through grants, awards and fellowships.
  • Undertake related charitable activities in support of these aims.

These objectives describe our intended areas of work. Grants, awards and fellowships will be announced separately when available.

The Open Courtyard

A place to gather.

Our logo imagines an open courtyard: a welcoming place for writers, readers and ideas. Its architectural form expresses our commitment to nurturing literature and making room for new voices.

It is an architectural interpretation, not a reproduction of a historical monument.

The flowing canopy

The canopy evokes poetry, imagination and the movement of language. Its rising, flowing shape suggests the freedom to explore and create.

The pillars

The two pillars express continuity and support: the care and encouragement that help literature grow.

The open space

The space between the pillars welcomes dialogue across languages, generations and backgrounds. It leaves room for different voices to meet.

The curved foundation

The curved foundation suggests shared gathering—through festivals, workshops, readings and recognition of literary work.

Colour and identity

A shared colour.
A distinct identity.

Mulberry creates a visual relationship with Kabita Live, our literary journal, while the Trust retains its own identity.

Whether the symbol sits above the name or alongside it, the meaning remains the same: an open courtyard for literature.
