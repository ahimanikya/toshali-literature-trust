Communicate With Us

The Toshali Literature Festival, hosted in Bhubaneswar, is a vibrant celebration of literature and the arts. With engaging panel discussions, poetry sessions, and cultural events, it brings together authors, thinkers, and creatives from across India. Organized by the Udaygiri Foundation, the festival aims to promote diverse voices, regional literature, and intellectual discourse. It serves as a platform for exploring ideas, storytelling, and the power of words in an evolving cultural landscape.

Experience inspiring talks from industry leaders, innovators, and visionaries who will share their expertise, success stories, and practical insights to empower your growth.
